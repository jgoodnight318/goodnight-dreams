The Podcast

Quiet Hours — Sleep Sound Machine

Full-length ambient audio for falling asleep, staying asleep, and blocking out the world. Rain, ocean waves, a crackling fireplace, forest wind, brown/pink/white noise, and three all-night 8-hour sessions.

Every episode is procedurally generated by an algorithmic DSP engine — shaped noise, filters, and envelopes — not sampled from a stock library and not an AI voice or music model. No dialogue, no music, no narration.
