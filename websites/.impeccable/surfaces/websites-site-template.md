Scope: generated one-page sites for local trade businesses (Cascade Web Design lane). Mode: Persuade. Audience: (1) the business owner receiving his preview cold — must feel "my company, done right"; (2) a stressed homeowner on a phone — must tap call within seconds. Proof material is thin by design (name, trade, city, phone, hours, sometimes license/est. year); no invented facts, no stock photos.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is the business's own truck door at highway scale — the trade's most trusted advertising object — refusing the stock-photo hero template and its gradient-SaaS opposite.
OWN-WORLD: Painted-enamel materiality. Deep enamel ground in a per-business color; cream lettering with signpainter shade-offset; gold accents for "Est./License"; hairline pinstripe rules; safety orange appears ONLY on call actions. Faces: Alfa Slab One (painted slab, business name), Barlow Semi Condensed (California signage vernacular, everything else), a script flourish used once at most.
STORY: Homeowner reads name/trade/city like a lettered truck from their own neighborhood, believes "established local pro," taps the giant painted call panel. Owner sees his name in paint and wants to keep it.
FIRST VIEWPORT (mobile-first): enamel ground full-bleed; top chip "Licensed & Insured" (+ license # when sourced); center: business name at door-lettering scale with shade offset, arced trade-and-city line beneath; bottom third: safety-orange CALL panel with the phone number at sign scale (tel:). Pinstripe frame.
FORM: truck-door lettering / fleet livery — position 1 of my ordered list; seed key 17cc7186; chosen by user criterion "whichever will get us paid."
SIGNATURE: one specular glint sweeps the call panel on load (sun on enamel); sections reveal as stacked painted panels while lettering shade deepens subtly on scroll.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
