## Description (1,050 chars)

Most small businesses waste 5–15 hours a week on work a $20/month automation could do. The problem isn't tools, it's knowing which process to automate first and exactly how.

Send me a description of how your business runs (or a 10-minute voice note) and I'll return an audit that lists every repeatable process, estimates hours per week and dollar value, flags which ones an AI step can handle (reading, sorting, drafting, extracting), and ranks them by payoff. The top item comes with a build-ready spec: trigger, steps, apps, and what could go wrong.

You can take the spec to anyone. Most clients have me build it (see my other gigs), and the audit fee is credited if you do.

What you get: a ranked audit (PDF + editable doc), ROI estimates with the assumptions shown, and a build-ready spec for your #1 process.
