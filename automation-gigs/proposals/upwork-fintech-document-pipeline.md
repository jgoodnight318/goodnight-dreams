ACCURACY.md in the repo is generated by actually running it, not typed by hand.

How I handle low-confidence extractions, since you asked specifically:

- Every field carries its own confidence, not just the document as a whole.
- A document is accepted only when classification confidence is at least 0.85, every extracted field is at least 0.80, and it passes all business rules. Anything else lands in a needs_review queue with the exact reasons attached (which field, what threshold, which rule), so a reviewer fixes one thing instead of re-reading the whole document.
- Fail closed by design: any exception anywhere in the pipeline routes the document to review with a summary. In finance a missing document is worse than a slow one, so silent drops are the failure I engineer against hardest.
- Thresholds live in one config block and are documented, so you can tune the accept/review split against your reviewers' capacity.
